KID DRACULA BITES ON!

Kid Dracula from the Kid Dracula Series!

Kid Dracula is the self-proclaimed Demon King, a son of Dracula and a main protagonist in a Kid Dracula series.
He has awoken from his sleep for a long time, and noticed that Galamoth wants to dare him to take a challenge with him.
Grabbing his father's cape, it's up to Kid Dracula to save the world and retake his throne. It is said that
Kid Dracula is unbelievably 10,009 years old according to Castlevania: Judgement, when Galamoth sends the Time Reaper from the future.


AI Options
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
KidDracula/KidDracula.def = Stronger AI
KidDracula/KidDracula_Moderate = Normal AI
KidDracula/KidDracula_Easy.def = Easy AI


Abilites
-----------------------------------------------------------
Dodge Twirl:                      B, F to roll right/F, B to roll left

Recovery Twirl:                   Hold F/B when knocked down

Air Dash:                         F, F/B, B while in air

Super Jump:                       D, U (Great for combos)

Throw:                            Hold F, and y+z while close to the opponent

Fireball:                     D, DF, F, b

Homing Projectile:            D, DF, F, c

Bat:                          D, DF, F, x

Iceball:                      D, DF, F, y

Exploder:                     D, DF, F, z
  
Random Helpers:               D, DB, B, a/b/c/x/y/z (Vampire Soccer cannot be activated for a short time)

-Frankie goes foward, and dashes towards the opponent, can be defeated

-Danny will jump roll and slash both sides, can be defeated

he eventually stands up quickly and runs away, can be defeated
