# AI Patches
AI Patches are modifications where you take a character, study their mechanics, and code the most effective Artificial Intelligence to cater to user's choice of difficulties.

In the MUGEN community, there are a vast of characters with varying AI. Sometimes there's either no AI at all, lackluster performance, or just needed a tiny spice to their playstyle. That's where AI Patchers (like me) come in.

Each one of these folders contain:
- A readme.md of each character
- Two files to automatically setup the match
-   .bat (Windows users)
-   .command (Linux/Mac users)

## If you're a Linux user
1. Open up terminal within the showcase folders
2. "chmod +x (character name).command" to compile
3. "(character name).comman" to run
